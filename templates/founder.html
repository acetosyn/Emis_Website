About the Founder

A Life Shaped by Vision, Learning, and Impact

Meet the man whose educational philosophy continues to shape EMIS with structure, balance, and purpose.

Dr. Kola Adeyemi is a visionary leader with a deep passion for education and human development. His background spans Communications, Public Relations, and Law, giving him a well-rounded perspective on leadership and meaningful impact.

As the founder of Epitome Model Islamic Schools, he has built an intentional system that brings together Western education and Islamic teachings in a way that feels balanced, structured, and purposeful.

The goal is not only academic success, but raising individuals who are grounded in values, disciplined in character, and conscious of purpose.
