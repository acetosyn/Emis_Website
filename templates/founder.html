About the Founder

A Life Shaped by Vision, Learning, and Impact

Dr. Kola Adeyemi is a visionary leader with a deep passion for education and human development. His background spans Communications, Public Relations, and Law, giving him a well-rounded perspective on leadership and impact.

As the founder of Epitome Model Islamic Schools, he has created something intentional — a system that brings together Western education and Islamic teachings in a way that feels balanced and purposeful.

The goal is not just academic success, but raising individuals who are grounded in values and aware of who they are.

Intentional leadership. Lasting influence.
