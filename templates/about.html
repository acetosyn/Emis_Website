Our Story

Building minds, shaping character, strengthening faith.

EMIS was built on the belief that education should not only inform the mind, but also refine character and strengthen faith. Our school exists to provide children with a learning environment where academic excellence and Islamic values grow side by side.

We serve learners across Creche, Nursery, Primary, Junior Secondary, and Senior Secondary levels. Through a thoughtful structure that includes Tahfeez and Islamiyyah, we help students grow into capable, disciplined, and morally grounded individuals.

Our Foundation

Vision, Mission & Core Purpose

Everything at EMIS is guided by a clear educational purpose rooted in faith, excellence, and service.

Our Vision

To nurture a generation of learners who excel academically, uphold strong Islamic values, and grow into responsible leaders in society.

Our Mission

To deliver a rich blend of Islamic and Western education in a disciplined, supportive, and inspiring learning environment that promotes knowledge, character, and faith.

Our Core Purpose

To shape children into confident, morally upright, knowledgeable, and purpose-driven individuals prepared for life, leadership, and service.
