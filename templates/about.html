About EMIS

Preparing young minds for a better future through faith and education.

Epitome Model Islamic Schools was established with the aim of preparing youth for a future where issues such as ethnicity, tribalism, and nepotism will not have a place.

Our school programme is part of a broader effort to build a society free from crisis, where the fear of God guides the actions of young people instead of the influence of ungodly societal pressures.

Our Foundation

Vision, Mission & Educational Purpose

EMIS is guided by a clear direction rooted in faith, total education, and a commitment to raising responsible future leaders.

Our Vision

Preparing for a nation where the fear of Allah will be paramount in the minds of leaders and mutual suspicion among tribes will be a thing of the past.

Our Mission

To reduce the level of illiteracy to the barest minimum through the provision of total education in Western and Islamic studies, sciences, languages, arts, and crafts.

Our Core Focus

To build a disciplined, knowledgeable, supportive, and God-conscious school community where students are prepared for excellence in life and service to society.

Our Aims

What EMIS is committed to achieving

Our aims reflect our commitment to academic excellence, holistic development, healthy learning, strong values, innovation, and wise school governance.

Maintain a school culture of excellence in teaching, achievement, innovation, and self-improvement
Maintain a supportive, healthy, and secured environment for learning and teaching
Deliver a balanced and holistic international education program centered on Islamic and Western education

Deliver enriching co/extra-curricular opportunities for students

Staff Community

A collaborative team building a positive school atmosphere.

At EMIS, staff members are very supportive of the school’s mission and proud to work in a diverse community representing different cultural backgrounds and nationalities.

In the same way students are encouraged to support one another, our staff work together in a Professional Learning Community, sharing ideas and improving teaching practice.

EMIS staff enjoy a work environment and set of conditions that contribute greatly to the school’s very positive atmosphere.
